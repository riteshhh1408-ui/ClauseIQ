ClauseIQ: Ask Any Document. Get Verified Answers

Problem:

People receive lengthy documents such as policies, contracts, rulebooks, and manuals.
Reading these documents is time-consuming.
As a result, users often miss important information.


Solution:

ClauseIQ is an AI-powered document intelligence platform.
Users upload a document.
They can ask questions in natural language.
The AI answers only from the uploaded document and provides citations.
The application can also summarize documents, detect important clauses, and compare two document versions.
